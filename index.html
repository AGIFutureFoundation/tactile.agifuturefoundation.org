Tactile — order is the product.

Assembly and Sequencing Agent — plans and monitors stepwise industrial or lab workflows.

A multi-step workflow only works if the steps happen in the right order, with the right inputs, and someone notices when one stalls. Tactile turns a process description into an ordered plan with checkpoints — then watches it run, reports progress and blockers, and audits it when you're done.
